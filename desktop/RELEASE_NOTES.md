## diyalamp 1.4.0

- Reopen the last workspace with its tab order, selected documents, and split width. Reselecting the current folder keeps the workspace in place.
- Use shorter, aligned workspace bars, responsive 20% sidebar defaults, and breadcrumbs that reveal the enclosing folder.
- Keep the active note or preview open as folders collapse, with stable row positions during folder transitions.
- Navigate with Back and Forward, capture Quick Tasks, and hide workspace content with Privacy Mode from the toolbar.
- Search filenames and note contents in the sidebar, or use inline Find with match counts and next and previous controls.
- Preview TXT, PDF, JPEG, PNG, GIF, and BMP alongside editable Markdown. Fit or zoom PDFs and images in either pane.
- See distinct linked-note counts and animated Incoming and Outgoing lists.
- Keep long tab titles readable in narrow panes and use consistent button, dialog, accent, and Pomodoro controls.
